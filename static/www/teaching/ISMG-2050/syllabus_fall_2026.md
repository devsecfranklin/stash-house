## Course Description

ISMG 2050 introduces students to the technology and problem-solving skills necessary for success both at school and in the business world. This hands-on course focuses on making data-driven decisions using spreadsheets, data analysis, and visualization tools. Students solve quantitative and strategic problems across statistics, accounting, finance, marketing, management, and information systems. We specifically utilize Microsoft Excel and Tableau to equip students with practical methodologies for the modern business landscape.

As a business core course, a grade of **C-** or better (70.00%) must be earned to satisfy Business graduation requirements and advance to higher-level business courses.

---

## Course Goals & Learning Objectives

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

- Obtain foundational and intermediate proficiency in Microsoft Excel.
- Construct formulas utilizing mathematical operators, logical functions, and lookup arrays.
- Present data effectively with professional formatting, dynamic charts, and PivotTables.
- Apply logic models, What-If analysis, and financial functions to business decisions.
- Master ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) processes using Excel Power Query.
- Obtain proficiency in Tableau to build interactive dashboards and visual stories from multidimensional datasets.

## AI Collaboration & Academic Integrity Policy

Artificial Intelligence tools (such as ChatGPT, Claude, Microsoft Copilot, and Gemini) are integral components of the modern analyst workflow. **Students are encouraged to actively use AI as a learning accelerator, debugging partner, and cognitive tutor.**

### Encouraged Uses

- **Function & Formula Deconstruction:** Asking AI to break down complex nested formulas (e.g., INDEX/XMATCH or multi-criteria IF statements) into plain English.
- **Syntax & Error Debugging:** Pasting broken formulas or error codes (#VALUE!, #REF!, #N/A) to identify syntax mistakes or array mismatches.
- **Visualization Design:** Brainstorming chart types, color palettes, and dashboard layout ideas.
- **Concept Review:** Prompting AI to generate practice questions and conceptual drills to prepare for exams.

### Prohibited Uses

- Submitting raw AI-generated files, spreadsheets, or dashboards without completing and understanding the work yourself.
- Using AI or external resources during closed-book, in-class midterm and final examinations.
- Submitting unverified AI outputs or misrepresenting another person's work as your own.

### AI Citation Requirement

When AI is consulted to help solve an assignment problem, include an inline disclosure note in your submission:

> *AI Disclosure: Used [Tool Name, e.g., ChatGPT / Gemini] to [Purpose, e.g., "debug nested XLOOKUP formula and explain array height errors"].*

If no AI was used: *AI Disclosure: None.*

## Course Schedule & Modular Breakdown

### Module 1: Creating a Worksheet and a Chart (Weeks 1 & 2)
- **Topics:** Ribbon navigation, grid mechanics, basic arithmetic functions, cell styles, proportional Pie Charts.
- **Hands-on Projects:** Real Estate Budget, Dynamic Updates, Style Application, Laptop Analysis.

### Module 2: Formulas, Functions, and Formatting (Weeks 3 & 4)
- **Topics:** Flash Fill, math operators, conditional formatting rules, page setup.
- **Hands-on Projects:** Salary Report, Cost Analysis, Customer Tracking, College Cost Calculator.

### Module 3: Large Worksheets, What-If Analysis, and Charting (Weeks 5 & 6)
- **Topics:** Absolute vs. Relative referencing ($A$1), nested IF logic, Sparklines, What-If analysis.
- **Hands-on Projects:** Financial Projections, Logic Tests, Census Data.
- **Assessment:** **Quiz 1 (Week 6).**

### Module 4: Financial Functions, Data Tables, and Amortization (Weeks 7 & 8)
- **Topics:** Time Value of Money (PMT, PV, FV), cash flow signs, amortization schedules, sheet protection.
- **Hands-on Projects:** Mortgage Payment Calculator, Loan Analysis, Retirement Planning.

### Module 5: Multiple Worksheets and Advanced Function Library (Weeks 9 & 10)
- **Topics:** Multi-sheet 3-D formulas, data consolidation, custom formatting codes.
- **Hands-on Projects:** Payroll Consolidation, Custom Codes, Multi-year Trends.

### Module 6: Creating, Sorting, and Querying Tables (Weeks 11 & 12)
- **Topics:** Structured table objects, XLOOKUP querying, Advanced AutoFilters.
- **Hands-on Projects:** Account Management, Table Logic.
- **Assessment:** **Midterm Examination (Week 12).**

### Module 7: Power Query (ETL), PivotTables, and Macros (Weeks 13 & 14)
- **Topics:** Power Query ETL data pipelines, PivotTables, PivotCharts, Macro automation.
- **Hands-on Projects:** Power Query Transformation Pipeline, Interactive Slicers.

### Module 8: Tableau Visual Analytics and Dashboards in Computer Lab (Weeks 15 & 16)
- **Topics:** Business School Computer Lab sessions: Dimensions vs. Measures, interactive filters, dashboard storytelling.
- **Deliverables:** **Tableau Capstone Project Due; Comprehensive Final Exam (Week 16).**
