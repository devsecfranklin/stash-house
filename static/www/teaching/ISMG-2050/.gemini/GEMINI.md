# ISMG 2050: Generative AI (Gemini & Ollama) Integration Plan

## 1. Course Context & Refresh Objectives
**Course:** ISMG 2050 - Business Problem Solving Tools [1, 2]
**Format:** Asynchronous, 16-week module structure (Canvas) [1-3]
**Current Focus:** Problem-solving and decision-making using Microsoft Excel and Tableau with real-world scenarios [4, 5].
**Goal:** Refresh the 16-week curriculum by introducing modules on generative AI (Gemini and Ollama) and advanced data management tools to foster a modern culture of exploration and inquiry in Data-Driven Business Problem Solving [6].

## 2. Integration Mapping (16-Week Schedule)
The following outlines where Gemini, Ollama, and new data tools can augment the existing syllabus.

### Phase 1: Foundational Excel & AI Assistance (Weeks 1-5)
*   **Current State:** Focuses on creating workbooks, basic/advanced formulas (Statistical, Logical, Lookup), and charts [7-10].
*   **Gemini/Ollama Integration:** Introduce Gemini as an "AI Tutor" for Excel. Teach students how to write prompts to troubleshoot complex nested `IF` functions, `VLOOKUP`/`XLOOKUP`, and mathematical order of operations [9].

### Phase 2: Data Management & Transformation (Weeks 6 & 11)
*   **Current State:** Week 6 focuses on Getting and Managing Data (Tables, PivotTables, Flash Fill, filtering) [7, 11]. Week 11 focuses heavily on Power Query for Extract, Transform, and Load (ETL) tasks [7, 12].
*   **Gemini/Ollama Integration:** Introduce additional data management tools here. Students can use Gemini to generate "M code" for Power Query, brainstorm data cleansing strategies, or explain complex dataset structures prior to importing [12]. 

### Phase 3: Advanced Analysis & AI Synergy (Week 12)
*   **Current State:** Focuses on Data Analysis Tools like Scenarios, Goal Seek, Solver, and Macros [7, 12].
*   **Gemini/Ollama Integration:** This is a prime week for a dedicated Generative AI module. Students can use Gemini to generate VBA code for Excel Macros [12], or to analyze the statistical outputs generated by the Analysis ToolPak [12].

### Phase 4: Tableau & AI-Driven Storytelling (Weeks 13-14)
*   **Current State:** Focuses on Tableau Fundamentals, data cleaning, custom visualizations, and creating interactive dashboards and stories [7, 13, 14].
*   **Gemini/Ollama Integration:** Teach students how to use Gemini and Ollama to brainstorm data "stories," design effective dashboard layouts, and write complex calculated fields in Tableau [13, 14]. 

## 3. Workload & Grading Considerations
When adding the new Gemini and Ollama modules, the following existing course constraints must be managed:

*   **Time Commitment:** Students are currently expected to dedicate 6 to 12 hours per week to the coursework [7, 15]. New AI modules should be balanced against existing Pause & Practice assignments and weekly projects [8-12, 14, 16-18].
*   **Coursera Certificate Integration:** Currently, students must complete the free Coursera course *"Data for Business Analysts using Microsoft Excel"* [19, 20]. These modules are completed in parallel during Weeks 2, 4, 5, 7, 9, and 12 [19, 21]. 
    *   *Action Item:* Decide whether to replace this Coursera requirement with internal Gemini/Ollama modules, or keep the Coursera certificate (which currently accounts for 10% of the final grade) and embed AI usage into the existing Excel projects [21-23].
*   **Late Policy:** Ensure students are aware that the strict 10%-per-day late penalty applies to any new AI-focused assignments, with a hard cutoff after 9 days [22, 24, 25].

## 4. Academic Honesty in the Age of AI
*   **Current Policy:** The syllabus strictly prohibits plagiarism, cheating, and submitting another's work as one's own [26-28]. 
*   *Action Item:* The syllabus must be explicitly updated to define "authorized" versus "unauthorized" use of Gemini and Ollama. Clear guidelines should be provided on how students must cite or disclose their use of AI tools when completing Excel formulas, Macros, and Tableau dashboards.
