Notes For Admins

When a player joins the server, they have almost no permissions. This is to prevent griefing and such. You will have to move them from "default" group to the "player" group once you are sure they are not insane, griefing, etc.

The command to change someone to player is:
/manuadd <playername> player
You can also demote a griefer like so:
/manuadd <playername> default
